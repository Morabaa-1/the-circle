Organising takes a lot of work

The organiser keeps track of every payment, follows up with people and reminds everyone whose turn it is.

With a notebook and messages

  • ✕You write down each payment by hand.
  • ✕You send messages to check who has paid.
  • ✕People ask you again and again whose turn it is.

With The Circle

  • ✓You see who has paid and who has not, on one screen.
  • ✓You see the full 10-month list of whose turn it is.
  • ✓You record each month when it is done.

How it works

Three simple steps, every month.

  1. 1

    Track contributions

    See which members have paid this month and which payments are still missing. Tap “Mark paid” when someone pays.

  2. 2

    See whose turn is next

    See the 10-month list. It shows who gets the €1,000 next, and who already had their turn.

  3. 3

    Record the monthly payout

    When everyone has paid, confirm that you gave the €1,000 to the right person. The next month then starts.

    Good to know: this only records what you did. It does not send or move any money.
