Our Savings Circle

10 friends save together. Each person pays €100 every month. Each month, one person gets all €1,000. After 10 months, everyone has had one turn.

How it works
  1. Every month, each friend pays €100 to the organiser.
  2. The organiser taps “Mark paid” when a friend pays.
  3. When all 10 have paid, there is €1,000.
  4. The organiser gives the €1,000 to one friend. The list below shows whose turn it is.
  5. A new month starts. Everyone pays again. After 10 months, everyone has had a turn.

The person who gets €1,000 also pays their €100 that month. That is why the total is €1,000.

The details

The numbers, the list of friends and who gets €1,000 each month.

This is a demo. All people and payments are made up. No real money is held or moved.
